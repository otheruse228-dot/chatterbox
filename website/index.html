How to install

Chatterbox is not in the Play Store, so your phone asks once whether you trust it. That is normal.

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If the phone asks, tap Settings and allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, open Chatterbox and sign up with your email.

Windows

  1. Click Download for Windows and open the setup file.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Finish the setup. Chatterbox stays in the tray so messages keep arriving.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no payments.

Does it work on iPhone or Mac?

Not yet. Chatterbox runs on Android phones (Android 7 or newer) and Windows 10 or 11 PCs.

Why does my phone or PC warn me when installing?

Apps that do not come from the Play Store or Microsoft Store always show this warning. Allow it once and Chatterbox installs normally. Updates come from inside the app after that.

Who can read my messages?

Only the people in that chat. Vanish messages are removed from the server once read, and locked chats never show their text in notifications. Details are on the privacy page.

Do I lose my chats when the app updates?

No. Chats are kept on the server and on your device, and updates never touch them.

When will it be on the Play Store?

We are preparing the Play Store release. Until then, download the app from this page; it updates itself, and you will not need to reinstall when it reaches the Play Store.

How do I delete my account?

Send a request through the feedback form above with the email you signed up with. Your account, messages, files and statuses are deleted within 30 days.

Who makes Chatterbox?

Chatterbox is an independent app built in India for friends, family and small teams. It is not affiliated with WhatsApp, Meta or any other messaging company.
