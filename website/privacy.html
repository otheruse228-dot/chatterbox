Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

In short: we collect only what is needed to deliver your messages and calls, we never sell your data, we show no ads, and you can ask for everything to be deleted.

1. Who we are

Chatterbox ("we", "us") is an independent messaging app for Android and Windows, available at chatterboxapp.in. This policy explains what we collect when you use the app or this website, why, and the choices you have. By using Chatterbox you agree to this policy.

2. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), your name and an optional profile photo.
  • Content you share: messages, photos, videos, files, voice notes, reactions, statuses and the list of people you chat with.
  • Activity: when a message was delivered and seen, your online status and last seen time (you can hide these in Settings → Privacy), and call records (who called whom, when, how long). Call audio and video go directly between devices and are not recorded or stored.
  • Device: a notification token so we can send you notifications, the app version and the platform (Android or Windows).
  • Feedback: what you write in the feedback form, plus a name or contact detail only if you give one.

We do not collect your contacts list, location, advertising identifiers or browsing history.

3. How we use it

  • To deliver messages, calls, statuses and notifications.
  • To keep your account secure and stop abuse.
  • To fix problems and improve the app, using feedback and basic error information.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not build profiles of you.

4. Who can see your content

  • Messages, files and calls: only the people in that chat.
  • Statuses: people you have a chat with, for 24 hours.
  • Your name and profile photo: other Chatterbox users.
  • Locked chats: notifications show only "New message", without the sender or text.

5. Where it is stored and who helps us

Your data is stored with service providers that process it only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering notifications.
  • Netlify: hosting this website.
  • GitHub: hosting the app download files.

These providers may store data on servers outside India. We use providers with recognised security practices, and data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

6. How long we keep it

  • Vanish messages: deleted from the server once they are read.
  • Statuses: shown for 24 hours, then deleted.
  • Messages deleted for everyone, or deleted by every person in the chat: removed from the server together with their files.
  • Everything else: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer.

7. Security

Access to every chat is restricted to its members on the server, files are private and opened through short-lived links, connections are encrypted, and app updates are checked for tampering before they install. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will inform you as required by law.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Change your name and photo, hide your online status and last seen, lock chats, and delete messages at any time in the app.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or the deletion of your account by writing to us through the feedback form on the home page with the email you signed up with.
  • You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

We respond to requests within 30 days, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

9. Children

Chatterbox is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 18, use it only with the permission of a parent or guardian. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it.

10. This website

This site does not use cookies or trackers. It remembers your light or dark choice in your own browser only. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the QR code tool from cdnjs, which receive the technical details any web request carries (such as your IP address). Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs for security.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.

12. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, write to us through the feedback form. Please leave a phone number or email so we can reply. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 days.
