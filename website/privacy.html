Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026.

What we store

Your email, your name and profile photo, the people you chat with, your messages, files, statuses and call history. They are stored so they reach the other person and stay available on your devices.

Who can see it

  • Messages, files and calls are visible only to the people in that chat.
  • Statuses are visible to the people you have a chat with, for 24 hours.
  • Your name and profile photo are visible to other Chatterbox users.
  • Vanish messages are deleted from the server once they are read.
  • When everyone in a chat deletes a message, it is removed from the server together with its file.

Where it is stored

On Supabase (database and file storage) and on your own devices. Notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; locked chats send notifications without the message text.

What we never do

No ads, no selling or sharing of your data, no tracking across other apps or websites.

Deleting your account

Contact the person who invited you to Chatterbox to have your account and data removed.
